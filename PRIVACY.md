# Embermine privacy notice

Embermine is a modified build of Firefox for Android, made by Ivy (https://ivy.yt). Mozilla does not make or run Embermine. Some Mozilla services are still used, as described below. This notice describes the default settings.

## What Embermine sends by default

- Requests for the pages you open, and the search terms you enter, go to those websites and to the search engine you chose.
- Some built-in lists, such as search engine lists and tracking protection lists, are downloaded from Mozilla's servers (Remote Settings). These downloads do not contain your browsing history.

## What Embermine does not send by default

- Telemetry is off. Usage and diagnostic data is not sent to Mozilla.
- Crash reports cannot be sent from this build. Crashes stay on your device, listed under About > Crashes.
- Mozilla's experiments (Nimbus) are not downloaded.
- Sponsored shortcuts, sponsored stories and Pocket stories are off.
- Safe Browsing is off unless the build includes its own Google Safe Browsing API key.

You can turn telemetry on or off in Settings > Data collection. A change to telemetry takes effect the next time Embermine starts. You can turn sponsored shortcuts, sponsored stories and Pocket stories on or off in Settings > Homepage.

## Mozilla services

- Sync needs a Mozilla account and is used only after you sign in. Your synced data is encrypted on your device before it is sent.
- Firefox Suggest sends what you type in the address bar to Mozilla's suggestion service while its suggestions are turned on. You can turn them off in Settings > Search.

Mozilla's privacy notice explains how Mozilla handles data for these services: https://www.mozilla.org/privacy/firefox/
